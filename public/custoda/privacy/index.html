Custoda privacy.

This page explains the privacy commitments guiding Custoda. It will be updated with final, app-specific practices before public release.

Pre-release notice

Custoda is still in development. The final policy will identify the exact information processed by the released app and any service providers it uses.

Our approach

ICANCREATE aims to collect only the information required to provide and improve Custoda. We do not describe information as private unless our technical practices support that promise.

Information and use

Before release, this section will list each category of information the app processes, why it is needed, where it is stored, and whether it is linked to you.

Service providers

Any analytics, hosting, crash-reporting, or other third-party services included in the released app will be identified here with links to their applicable policies.

Retention and deletion

The final policy will explain how long information is retained and how users can request access, correction, export, or deletion where applicable.

Children's privacy

Custoda is intended for parents and guardians. The final product and policy will describe any handling of information relating to children before the app becomes available.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to support@icancreate.studio.
